# Client Orders Journal

## Orders
1. Click **New order**. On the right, enter the title, client, type, price, prepayment, deadline and status.
2. Attach the project folder in **Folder**; the button next to it opens it in Explorer.
3. Click **Save**. The order appears in the table.
4. Record later payments with **Add payment**. The balance is calculated automatically.
5. Active orders past their deadline are highlighted in red. Click the "Overdue" card to show only those.
6. Narrow the list with search and the status and client filters.

## Invoice
Select an order and click **Invoice (PDF)**. The document includes the designer's name, client, payments and balance due.
Change the designer name in **Settings > Designer name**.

## Clients
Keep name, phone, Telegram and notes. Selecting a client lists all their orders; double-click to open one.

## Income
Money received over the last 12 months (prepayments and payments) is shown as a bar chart and by work type.
**Export to Excel** writes orders, clients, payments and monthly income to one workbook.

Data is stored in `%APPDATA%\BahtiyorSoft\buyurtmalar-daftari\buyurtmalar.db`. Copy this file to back it up.
